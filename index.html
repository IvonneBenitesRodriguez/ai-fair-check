Exercise 1 · Algorithm

Many ways to 2

Most people think of 1 + 1 = 2 right away. But are there other ways? Write a calculation that equals 2.

= 2?

All correct ways:

  • 1 + 1

And the AI?

An AI reads millions of texts from the internet. If almost everyone writes “1 + 1”, the AI might only show 1 + 1, as if it were the only answer.

The AI does not think for itself. It repeats what many people have written, including their mistakes.
